FAQ

Do I need technical knowledge?

No. Just talk to the AI in plain language. Explain what you need, and it will guide you through everything.

Will it delete my files?

No. AI Rescue USB automatically backs up before doing anything risky. It will always ask for confirmation before modifying your data.

Can it fix a computer that won't boot?

Yes. That's exactly what it's designed for. It can repair Windows, Linux, and BSD boot problems automatically.

Does it work offline?

Yes. All core features work without internet. Internet is only needed for downloading OS images or driver updates.

What if something goes wrong?

Every risky operation creates a backup first. If anything fails, you can restore the backup. The AI will guide you through recovery.
